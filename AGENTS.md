# Fees: earn openly, never promise free

Fully Sorted is a business and is allowed to make money, including on sales. Fairness comes from disclosure, not from promising there will never be a fee. These rules cover page copy, metadata, `public/llms.txt`, emails, specs and seed data.

1. **Never claim the absence of a fee.** Banned in any form: "no fee on the sale", "no commission", "zero commission", "no buyer's premium", "no percentage of the sale", "we take nothing", "nothing more when it sells", "free forever". Say what something costs, not what it doesn't. Describing other marketplaces' fees in research is fine.
2. **Every fee is shown before anyone pays,** in plain numbers, on the page where they pay, and on `/pricing`. No fee appears for the first time at checkout or after.
3. **No retroactive fees.** A new or changed fee is posted on `/pricing` and `/trust#how-we-make-money` before it starts, and never applies to a listing, booking or membership already paid for. The terms already say this; keep them saying it.
4. **Keep promises already made.** The first-100 free listings, founding members' free directory listing for life, and free membership stay as written.
5. **No junk fees.** Nothing to message, nothing to cancel, nothing to join, nothing added to the price a seller or provider quotes a buyer.
6. **Don't invent or change a price, fee or plan without Chris.** Copy describes the fees that exist; it never commits to new ones or rules them out.
